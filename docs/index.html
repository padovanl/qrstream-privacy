QRStream Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

QRStream transfers files between two devices by encoding them into a sequence of QR codes on one screen and scanning them back with the other device's camera. Transfers work entirely offline and never use an internet connection.

This policy covers both QRStream apps: the Android app on Google Play and the Windows app on the Microsoft Store. They are two halves of the same product and handle data the same way, with one difference noted under Purchases: the Android app talks to Google Play Billing, and the Windows app makes no network requests of any kind.

Data collection

QRStream does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no user accounts.

Camera permission

QRStream requests camera access - the CAMERA permission on Android, webcam access on Windows - solely to scan the QR codes shown by the sending device, in real time, in order to reassemble the transferred file. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device and are never saved, uploaded, or shared. No photo or video is stored beyond the momentary in-memory processing needed to decode a QR code.

Files you transfer

Files you select to send, and files you receive, are stored only locally on your device (the app's private storage while a transfer is in progress, and your device's Downloads folder once a transfer completes; on Windows, the folder you choose). QRStream never uploads these files anywhere.

Purchases

Sending files above 500KB requires a one-time in-app purchase, made in the Android app. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing - QRStream never sees or stores your payment details. The Windows app has no purchase flow at all: it is unlocked by a code generated on the phone, so it never contacts Google, or any other server. The app only stores, locally on your device, whether that purchase was made, so it can be restored automatically if you reinstall the app on an account that already owns it.

Third parties

QRStream does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share. The one exception is Google Play Billing, which processes purchases under Google's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

QRStream does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since it collects no information at all.
